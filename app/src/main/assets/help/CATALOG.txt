Local call catalog

The catalog records caller information exposed by Meet, attempt timestamps, and whether the decline action was sent or failed. Caller identities and call outcomes remain unverified.

Up to 500 recent attempts within 90 days are retained. Older records are pruned on catalog access or write. QuietGate cannot reconstruct caller identities from before the catalog was enabled.

Categories and notes

Tap a record to edit its category and notes. Categories are your assessments; they do not change protection rules or verify scam claims.

Import and export

Use Rejected-call catalog to import or export JSON. Imports merge with existing records. Duplicate records keep existing edits, and imported claims remain labeled.

Supported input includes versioned QuietGate JSON and legacy array exports, up to 2 MiB and 5,000 input records. Invalid, expired, and excessively future-dated records are skipped. The retained result remains limited to 500 recent entries.

Exports contain retained identities, categories, and notes. Choose a trusted destination. A cloud document provider may upload the file. Clear catalog and Clear local history remove the app's copy; exported copies must be deleted separately.
