QuietGate's purpose

QuietGate helps reduce unwanted Google Meet calls. It compares an email address or phone number exposed by a Meet notification with contacts saved on this device and fresh contact snapshots from Google accounts you choose to connect.

If the identifier is missing or has no saved match, QuietGate attempts the Decline or Reject action supplied by Meet. Quiet mode can dismiss related missed-call alerts.

Limitations

QuietGate is experimental software. Caller information is not authenticated. A saved match does not prove that a caller is trustworthy, and a rejected attempt does not prove that a caller is a scammer.

The app depends on Meet exposing a usable notification and an English Decline or Reject action. A call may ring or display a popup before the app can act. QuietGate cannot independently confirm that Meet ended the call.

QuietGate does not block cellular calls, reveal hidden account information, or obtain a caller's IP address or location. Android restrictions, work profiles, reboots, force-stops, and changes to Meet can affect operation.
