Required for protection

Contacts: Read saved email addresses and phone numbers available through Android's Contacts provider. QuietGate FOSS does not edit contacts or connect to a Google account. Contacts synced by another app may be available through the device provider.

Notification access: Android grants broad access to notifications. QuietGate FOSS filters its processing to Meet, reads exposed caller information and action buttons, and can dismiss matched missed-call alerts.

Shizuku authorization: Check whether Shizuku is running and authorizes QuietGate FOSS. This remains a readiness condition; no privileged Meet call-control API is used. The FOSS edition needs its own authorization when installed beside the Google edition.

Status and system access

Notification permission: Show protection readiness and the grace countdown. Android 13 and later require this grant before enabling protection.

Foreground service and special use: Maintain the visible protection status service. Android can still stop or delay background work.

Network state: Check active Wi-Fi for protection readiness and the grace timer. This permission does not let the app open Internet connections. No SSID, IP address, or hardware address is collected.

Boot completed: Recheck the saved deadline and request status restoration where Android permits it.

Optional file access

Android's document picker grants access to files selected for catalog import, incident attachments, and export. No broad storage permission is requested. A selected cloud document provider may use its own network access to transfer those files.

QuietGate FOSS requests no Internet, Google account, microphone, audio-recording, location, phone-state, system call-log, or overlay permission. Root is not required. Active Wi-Fi is still required to enable protection; losing Wi-Fi or authorized Shizuku starts the existing 24-hour grace period.
