About QuietGate FOSS

QuietGate FOSS helps reduce unwanted Google Meet calls using contacts available on this Android device. It attempts Meet's exposed Decline or Reject action when no saved match is available.

This edition contains no Google Play services library, Google account connection, or background contact-refresh client. It requests no Internet permission.

QuietGate code and original assets are MIT-licensed. Bundled Shizuku client libraries are MIT-licensed; AndroidX Annotation is Apache-2.0. Google Meet is an external proprietary app and is not included in QuietGate FOSS. F-Droid inclusion has not been approved.
