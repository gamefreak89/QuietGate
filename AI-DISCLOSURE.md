- Java: Android application and instrumentation code.
- XML/vector XML: manifest, resources and icon assets.

## Development assistance

This project was developed iteratively with OpenAI Codex, an AI coding assistant based on GPT-6 in this session, with user-directed specifications and real-device feedback. Earlier user-supplied design/fix documents were described as Gemini-generated. This provenance is based on the conversation, not an independent verification of those documents' origin.

The exact model snapshot used across all earlier turns is not available and is not claimed. A ChatGPT subscription tier does not identify the exact model used. No AI/LLM is bundled with or invoked by QuietGate at runtime: matching and categorization are conventional local code, not AI fraud classification.

AI assistance does not establish correctness, ownership of third-party materials, privacy certification or security assurance. Verification status and remaining limitations are documented in DEVICE-VERIFICATION.md.
